# Getting Started

Use this quick checklist to get fully set up:

1. Sign in with Discord
2. Link your Steam account
3. Join or create a community
4. Enable push notifications (optional)

## 1. Sign In With Discord

Community Voting uses Discord for authentication. Click **Sign in** and authorize with your Discord account — no separate registration is required.

## 2. Link Your Steam Library

To sync your games with Community Voting, link your Steam account from [Profile](/profile).

<DocsAlert type="note">
        To sync your Steam library, your game details visibility <strong>must</strong> be set to public in your Steam privacy settings.
</DocsAlert>

## 3. Join A Community

Communities are invitation-only. To join an existing community, ask a member to share an invite link with you. Invite links look like:

```
/community/{communityId}/join/{inviteId}
```

Follow the link, sign in if prompted, and click **Join Community**. If the invite specifies a membership duration, you will see a notice explaining when your temporary membership expires — a moderator can make it permanent at any time.

Alternatively, [create your own community](/community/create) and invite others.

## 4. Enable Push Notifications (Optional)

Click the **notification bell** in the navigation bar and select **Enable notifications** to receive push alerts for new voting sessions, vote reminders, and session results. You can manage notification preferences from [your profile](/profile) at any time.
